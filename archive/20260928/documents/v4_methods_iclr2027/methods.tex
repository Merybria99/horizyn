\section{Method}
\label{sec:paper-v4-method}

\subsection{Problem formulation}
\label{sec:problem-formulation}
Let $\mathcal R$ and $\mathcal E$ denote reaction and enzyme sets. Observed
associations form a many-to-many bipartite graph
$\mathcal D\subseteq\mathcal R\times\mathcal E$. We learn enzyme--reaction
compatibility by independently mapping both modalities to a shared space.
The complete reaction and enzyme encoders $f_R,f_E$ give normalized outputs
$\mathbf{z}_r=f_R(r)$ and $\mathbf{z}_e=f_E(e)$, yielding the cosine score
$S(r,e)=\mathbf{z}_r^\top\mathbf{z}_e\in[-1,1]$.

The same score supports $E\!\to\!R$ retrieval over reaction candidates
$\mathcal C_R(e)\subseteq\mathcal R$ and $R\!\to\!E$ retrieval over enzyme
candidates $\mathcal C_E(r)\subseteq\mathcal E$. Relevant candidates are each
query's observed neighbors in the test graph, allowing multiple correct
results. Query sets, candidate pools, and positive neighborhoods can differ
between directions. Learning uses only the training graph
$\vD\subseteq\vR\times\vE$.

\method{} first aligns the encoders, then refines their embeddings over
the complete training graph before adding training-reaction coordinates.

\subsection{Reaction encoder}
\label{sec:reaction-encoder}
The reaction encoder combines frozen ReactionT5v2, Uni-Mol2, and ChIRo features
\citep{sagawa2023reactiont5,ji2024unimol2,adams2022chiro} with a
617-dimensional chemistry descriptor computed from participant SMILES.
This descriptor concatenates averaged Morgan fingerprints (512 coordinates),
four aggregates of 16 molecular properties (64), participant-set statistics
(4), and cofactor indicators (37). It supplies explicit substructure,
physicochemical, and cofactor cues alongside pretrained embeddings.
``Fixed'' means its construction is not optimized during retrieval training:
property normalization and the cofactor vocabulary are fitted on training
reactions and frozen; its neural projection is learned
(Appendix~\ref{app:v4-chemistry}).

For each molecular modality $m\in\{\mathrm{UniMol2},\mathrm{ChIRo}\}$,
independent attention poolers summarize reactant and product embeddings as
weighted means $\mathbf{p}_-^m$ and $\mathbf{p}_+^m$. Their concatenation
$\mathbf{c}_r^m=[\mathbf{p}_-^m;\mathbf{p}_+^m;\mathbf{p}_+^m-\mathbf{p}_-^m;|\mathbf{p}_+^m-\mathbf{p}_-^m|]$ retains both summaries
alongside their signed and absolute differences. Independent MLPs map these
two compositions, the ReactionT5v2 embedding, and the chemistry descriptor
to four 512-dimensional tokens. Modality attention averages available tokens
with learned weights; an output MLP and $\ell_2$ normalization yield $\mathbf{b}_r$.

EnzymeMap supplies physical reactant and product sides. ReactZyme supplies
an unordered participant set, copied to both poolers. Their parameters
differ, so $\mathbf{p}_-^m$ and $\mathbf{p}_+^m$ can differ without representing an observed
reaction direction. The chemistry descriptor combines participants
irrespective of side. Appendix~\ref{sec:v4-reaction} specifies the encoder.
