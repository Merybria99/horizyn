\myparagraph{Biological supervision (ablation only)}
The proposed method excludes this loss. In the ablation, we supervise the
residual heads with EC hierarchy, cofactor-associated
categories, and coarse bond-change annotations on training enzymes and reactions.

For category $c$, let $d_c^{\mathrm{within}}$ and $d_c^{\mathrm{background}}$
be confidence-weighted mean cosine distances between distinct annotated
members and from members to annotated nonmembers of the same family,
respectively. We define
\begin{equation}
 \ell_c=\bar w_c\left[m+d_c^{\mathrm{within}}
                       -d_c^{\mathrm{background}}\right]_+,\qquad m=0.1,
 \label{eq:ablation-relative-biology}
\end{equation}
Here $\bar w_c$ is the mean confidence of within-category annotation pairs.
Category-weighted averaging, followed by equal averaging over enzymes and
reactions, defines $\mathcal L_f$ (Appendix~\ref{sec:v4-biology}). We optimize
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal L_{\mathrm{Bio}}^{(2)}=\mathcal L_{\mathrm{CE}}^{(2)}+10\mathcal L_{\mathrm{id}}
       +\frac13\sum_{f\in\vFamilies}\mathcal L_f.
 \label{eq:ablation-phase2-total}
\end{equation}
The families receive equal weight; missing annotations contribute no evidence.
Biological gradients update only the residual heads. Annotations are used
only during training.
