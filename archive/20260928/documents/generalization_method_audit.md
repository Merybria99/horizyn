# Frozen generalization method: independent audit

**Assessment: share with caveats.** The reviewed implementation supports a training-only, independently encodable retrieval method. It does not establish prospective biological discovery or independence from all prior data exposure. This audit covers methodology and provenance, not the new held-out results; no new held-out scores were inspected or used to revise the method.

## What the method establishes

The selected method adds two residual MLPs to one frozen F3 checkpoint and mixes their cosine score with training-association anchor similarity, at weights 0.75 and 0.25. Each anchor coordinate represents a training reaction. An enzyme retrieves 32 training proteins, transfers their known reaction associations using the maximum neighbor weight per reaction, and normalizes that vector. A reaction independently retrieves 16 training reactions in centered chemical-feature space. Centers and adjacency use only training rows. Validation chooses parameters/checkpoints but does not contribute anchor associations.

This is **supervised transfer through a fixed training dictionary**, in addition to learned residual encoders. It is legitimate use of training labels, including when a candidate enzyme itself occurs in that dictionary. It should be described explicitly: the model retains training examples and their associations at inference. It is not a compact replacement consisting solely of two 512-dimensional neural towers.

Endpoint independence holds: an enzyme representation does not depend on the requested reaction or current candidate pool; a reaction representation does not depend on candidate enzymes. The concatenated dot product implements the fixed mixture. The sparse index preserves this score. The Reaction-Sim single-seed representation has 7,489 coordinates, with sparse anchor storage available; dictionary memory and encoding cost should accompany deployment claims.

No direct held-out label fitting was found in these paths. The [artifact audit](../../../runs/generalization_20260919_2251/production_guard_review/actual_artifact_audit.json) verified all 21 bundles, three training catalogs, and five inference receipts. The [implementation amendment](../../../runs/generalization_20260919_2251/implementation_amendments.json) records subsequent input/provenance guards, with unchanged encoding/scoring mathematics and unchanged freeze, data and checkpoints. These checks do not independently establish the absence of overlap in foundation-model or other upstream pretraining.

## Limits that change interpretation

| Concern | Finding and reporting requirement |
|---|---|
| Training memory and repeated sequences | Exact or close training neighbors can transfer their associations directly. Repeated sequences under different IDs can occupy several neighbor slots; maximum pooling prevents additive duplication of one reaction weight, but does not eliminate neighbor crowding. Report exact-sequence and chemistry overlap separately from held-out pair membership. |
| Novelty definitions | Validation `unseen_reaction` means absence from training reaction IDs. For enzyme-to-reaction metrics it restricts positive edges while retaining the full reaction candidate pool; an enzyme may appear in both seen and unseen strata. This is not automatically an unseen-enzyme or remote-homology test. |
| Incomplete association labels | Full-graph contrastive training includes every known positive in the target distributions, avoiding contradictions among recorded positives. Unrecorded true activities can still be penalized. Do not claim elimination of all false negatives or learning experimentally verified inactivity. |
| Repeated validation selection | Hubness, linear, anchor, graph and composition screens reused standard validation. Final composition chose the mean across three residual seeds with aggregate/unseen safeguards. Selection can still overfit validation; its bootstrap explicitly lacks selection adjustment. The frozen test comparison carries the confirmatory weight. |
| Split transfer | Shared recipe parameters were chosen on Reaction-Sim validation and transferred to Enzyme-Sim and Time. Each split still chooses its own residual checkpoint using that split's validation. Describe this as shared hyperparameters with per-split training/checkpoint selection, not zero-selection transfer. |
| Seed replication | Seeds 42/17/73 vary residual initialization while sharing each split's frozen F3 checkpoint, training data and deterministic anchor dictionary. They quantify residual-head variability, not end-to-end retraining uncertainty. The three benchmark splits also share the underlying source domain. The fixed ensemble remains secondary. |
| Historical exposure | The frozen recipe explicitly acknowledges that prior repository test reports and historical Case1 rankings informed diagnosis. New recipe selection excluded new test/Case1 scores; the overall research process was not completely blind or prospectively preregistered. |
| Biochemical scope | Participant self-reactions preserve the supplied molecule collection but discard assignment to reactant versus product sides. Retrieval does not establish directionality, kinetics, expression, cofactor dependence, activity under dietary conditions, or successful new wet-lab catalysis. |

## Metrics and comparator fairness

The primary metric is **all-positive macro reciprocal rank**: average `1/rank` over every known positive for each query, then average queries. It differs from first-positive MRR: positive ranks 1 and 2 give 0.75 versus 1.0. Its per-query maximum with `m` positives is `H_m/m`, so it should not be compared across panels with different positive multiplicities as though its ceiling were uniformly one. Hit@k means at least one known positive, not recall of all positives. Keep first-positive MRR and both directions visible, with candidate and positive counts.

The main architectural comparison must use the same exact parent F3 checkpoint, training-matched features, candidate order, positive graph, stable tie rule and FP64-accumulation/FP32-output score contract. Native-FP32 baseline sensitivity is a separate declared control because near ties can move ranks. Do not credit the new model for correcting a baseline-only input bug. Report historical versus matched inputs separately for F3 epoch29 and CIRCEv2 epoch23. The final trained residual is on F3; CIRCEv2 is a comparator, not a independently retrained improved backbone in this campaign.

Case1 fixes are supported by training semantics: ReactZyme F3 was trained on complete participant collections encoded `S>>S`; historical query extraction omitted the product from molecular pooling and used a different T5 format. Historical EnzymeCAGE-trained F3 epoch06 used directional training inputs and is a different lineage. Its historical P450 results cannot be relabeled as current ReactZyme F3. Likewise, raw ranking and the EnzymeCAGE reaction/homology prior are different protocols; the P450-finetuned released comparator has additional supervised exposure.

Published TIGER/EnzymeCAGE values should remain explicitly literature or release-protocol context unless exact checkpoint, split assets, candidate hashes, metric definitions and preprocessing are independently matched. Local all-positive MRR implementation does not resolve underspecified paper prose or guarantee paper-number parity.

## External biological evidence

**Case1:** 144 aliases collapse to 123 exact sequences. The primary endpoint is recovery of 12 source-checked paper catalysts; 24 paper-plus-patent positives are secondary. The 81 broad workbook-active versus 42 non-detect-only labels are assay-conditional, with cross-assay conflicts excluded from the negative class. Workbook dietary priority ranks are not activity ground truth. This is one retrospective reaction, not 123 independent experiments. The recorded MMseqs family audit helps characterize training exposure; no retrieved hit means unknown under that search, not proven absence of homologs. See the [Case1 evidence audit](../../../runs/generalization_20260919_2251/case1_audit/README.md).

**P450:** retain the released 191 queries, 490 IDs, 487 unique sequences and 318 known-positive edges. Unlisted pairs are not verified failures. No full participant set exactly matches training, but 28 queries have both panel molecules inside larger training sets. Current Reaction-Sim training contains 316 candidate IDs with identical sequences. The frozen stricter stratum has 126 queries with neither a containing chemical set nor any known-positive exact sequence in training; all 490 candidates remain ranked. This does not establish remote-homology novelty or absence from pretraining. Sequence deduplication would be a separate sensitivity protocol. ## Checks before final findings

1. Verify every final score receipt against its bundle, feature receipt, catalog order and immutable freeze; report missing or failed runs explicitly. Preserve seed42 as primary even if a diagnostic wins.
2. Present each official split and direction separately, together with all prespecified controls, first-positive metrics, candidate counts and multi-positive counts. Keep historical/matched preprocessing effects separate from the incremental method effect.
3. Preserve exact-overlap and homology caveats; do not call the full external panel wholly unseen. Official-split sequence-level duplicate/overlap counts have not been independently established by this audit. Do not introduce a new selection rule or change the frozen method in response to those diagnostics.
4. On P450, retain the predefined 163 chemistry-absent and 126 joint-absence strata and fixed reaction-row permutation diagnostic. A strong generic enzyme prior must not be mistaken for conditioning on the requested reaction. On Case1, show actual recovered literature catalysts and their evidence tiers; there is no single-query permutation test or candidate-IID confidence interval.
5. Label query-bootstrap intervals as conditional, with protein/reaction dependence and multiplicity uncorrected. Residual-seed spread and query sampling uncertainty measure different things. Validation intervals additionally ignore model selection.
6. Separate supported retrospective retrieval gains from biological interpretation. No new wet-lab experiment was conducted; activity, dietary suitability, and general novel-enzyme discovery remain unestablished.
