# Research documents

Current architecture and usage are documented in
[PIPELINES.md](../docs/PIPELINES.md). The maintained pipeline families are
V4, F3, and CIRCEv2; V4 uses dictionary-free scoring and no biological-label
auxiliary loss.

Previous campaign notes, biology/dictionary experiments, ablation reports,
figures, and early LaTeX drafts are preserved together in
[`archive/20260928/documents`](../archive/20260928/documents/). They are dated
research evidence, not instructions for the current pipeline. The original
draft bundles retain their sources and rendered PDFs.

Use the [current manuscript](../../-ICLR2027---Enzyme-Reaction-Retrieval/)
for the paper, [BASELINES.md](../docs/BASELINES.md) for comparator sources
and results, and [findings.md](../findings.md) for the chronological experiment
record. Moving these notes did not remove their underlying results or models.
